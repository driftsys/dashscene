# dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --timestats

Collected over a 15 s window, with the app drawing throughout.
`statsStart` and `statsEnd` below are the window the counters
actually covered — check they are 15 s apart. `-clear` is what
bounds it; `-enable` alone does not reset an already-enabled
SurfaceFlinger, and the dump then describes an unknown earlier interval.

`totalFrames` and `missedFrames` are the headline. The
`presentToPresent` histogram is the frame interval as the display saw
it, and `frameDuration` is how long each frame took to produce; the
per-layer jank payloads below them attribute misses to a layer.

This is the compositor's view and it is vendor-neutral. It says nothing
about where GPU time went inside a frame — that needs the Perfetto
trace beside this file, and vendor counters for the last step.
