# dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --timestats

Constructed to carry TWO blocks whose layerName both name the package — a
container layer and its (BLAST) child, the shape gpu-capture.sh's own layer
discovery already prefers the second of. read_timestats must take the
(BLAST) block's totalFrames (150) and not the container's (999), whichever
sorts first in the dump.
